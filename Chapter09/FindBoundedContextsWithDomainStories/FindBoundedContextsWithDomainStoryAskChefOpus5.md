# Answer

Four bounded contexts fall out of this six-sentence story: **Meal Planning** (1–2), **Cook Assistance** (3, 4, 6a), **Meal Preparation** (5a), and **Meal Sharing** (5b, 6b).

The most useful finding is in the reconciliation at the end: the "Chef" in this story is the same named responder that appears alongside *Community Cook* and the *Grandma Avatar* on the Community Cooking board elsewhere in this project — and here it's shown answering a *planning*-time request rather than a cooking catastrophe. That's independent evidence the same "Cook Assistance" context serves multiple trigger points and multiple responder types, so I recommended reusing that established name rather than minting a new one like "Ask a Chef."

Two calls are genuinely contested and worth a quick answer from the team:
- Does the shared post credit the Chef, or is the thanks private? That decides whether sentence 6 is one act or two.
- Is a professional Chef consultation different enough (cost, scheduling) from peer/AI help to eventually split Cook Assistance along a *responder-kind* axis, separate from the urgency-based split already raised in the invariants sheet?
